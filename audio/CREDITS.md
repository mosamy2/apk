# Audio credits

- `fx_birds.mp3` — real morning birdsong recording, trimmed to a 45 s seamless loop (3 s crossfade) and
  loudness-normalised from `public/sounds/animals/birds.mp3` in
  [remvze/moodist](https://github.com/remvze/moodist) (MIT repo). Per that repo's README the sounds come from
  third-party providers under the Pixabay Content License or CC0 — both allow free use inside an app, no attribution required.
- `fx_soft.mp3` — original synthesised chime generated by `tools/make_soft.py`.
