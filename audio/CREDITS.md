# Audio credits

- `fx_birds.mp3` — real morning birdsong recording, trimmed to a 45 s seamless loop (3 s crossfade) and
  loudness-normalised from `public/sounds/animals/birds.mp3` in
  [remvze/moodist](https://github.com/remvze/moodist) (MIT repo). Per that repo's README the sounds come from
  third-party providers under the Pixabay Content License or CC0 — both allow free use inside an app, no attribution required.
- `fx_soft.mp3` — original synthesised chime generated by `tools/make_soft.py`.
- Garden sound effects (`fx_water`, `fx_grow`, `fx_bloom`, `fx_harvest`, `fx_plant`, `fx_tap`, `fx_empty`, `fx_petbutterfly`, `fx_petladybug`, `fx_petbunny`) —
  trimmed/loudness-normalised from [wyzowl/sfx-kit](https://github.com/wyzowl/sfx-kit) (CC0 1.0, no attribution required):
  `transform-fill`, `transform-grow`, `shimmer-medium-sparkle`, `sting-warm-short`, `pop-large-bubble`, `pop-medium-bubble`,
  `pop-small-out`, `shimmer-short-soft`, `pop-small-bubble`, `move-spring`.
- `fx_petbird`, `fx_petbee` — short clips of `animals/birds.mp3` and `animals/beehive.mp3` from
  [remvze/moodist](https://github.com/remvze/moodist) (MIT repo; sounds under Pixabay Content License or CC0).
